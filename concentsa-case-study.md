# ConcentSA SEO Case Study

**Market:** Saudi Arabia  
**Sector:** Commercial B2B Furniture  
**Engagement:** International program delivered as part of a cross-market growth team  
**Role:** Darrell Locke - SEO & Content Strategist

## Team-Delivered Results

- 3.49 million search impressions over 16 months
- 39,400 commercial clicks
- #1-#2 organic positions across KSA
- Seven commercial verticals developed under one domain

## Challenge

Buyers researched seven distinct commercial verticals - restaurants, cafes, universities, schools, institutes, offices, and storage - each with different intent, SERP patterns, and decision committees.

## My Contribution

- Seven dedicated content silos, each built as a complete entity graph
- Image-search optimization using alt text, schema, and product data
- Answer-first content blocks designed to earn AI Overview citations
- Wholesale and local layering that combined B2B intent with city-level signals

Metrics are reported in the accompanying portfolio from Google Search Console and third-party tools. These were team outcomes, not individual results.
