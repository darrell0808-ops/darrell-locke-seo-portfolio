# Nasertrans SEO Case Study

**Market:** Egypt  
**Sector:** Logistics & Moving  
**Comparison:** Six-month period versus the preceding six months  
**Role:** Darrell Locke - SEO & Content Strategist within a growth team

## Team-Delivered Results

- Clicks: 719 to 2,570 - a 257% increase
- Impressions: 85,900 to 210,000 - a 144% increase
- CTR: 0.8% to 1.2% - a 50% improvement
- Average position: 15.7 to 9.5 - an improvement of 6.2 positions
- Google AI Overview visibility on three commercial queries
